Frequently asked questions

Does submitting this form book a ticket?

No. It only prepares a WhatsApp enquiry. A ticket is confirmed only after the authorised booking process is completed and confirmation is received.

Are fares and availability guaranteed?

No. Availability, fare, applicable charges and rules can change. These should be checked at the time of booking.

Is TRT RAILWAY an official railway website?

This is a customer information website for TRT RAILWAY. It is not an official Indian Railways or IRCTC website and does not imply endorsement by them.
